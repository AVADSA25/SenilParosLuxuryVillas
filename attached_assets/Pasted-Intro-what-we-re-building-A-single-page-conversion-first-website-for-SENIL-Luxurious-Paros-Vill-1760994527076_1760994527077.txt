Intro (what we’re building)
A single-page, conversion-first website for SENIL — Luxurious Paros Villas. It must feel quiet-luxury, load fast, and generate qualified leads. Core features: first-visit logo micro-intro, hero + full-bleed horizon video, sticky key-facts band, three villa cards (Top/Middle/Bottom) with floor-plans, design/amenities sections, map + distances, ownership timeline, brochure download gate (email via Resend + reCAPTCHA v3), contact & legal. Brand is minimal: Ash Stone, Olive Slate, Deep Graphite; type is Newsreader (display) + Inter (UI/body). Eight days to V1.
